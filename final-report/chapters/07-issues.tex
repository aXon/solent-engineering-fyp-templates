\chapter{Issues Arising from Implementation and Testing}
\label{ch:issues}

\begin{guidance}[What this chapter does]
Problems happen in every project; how you dealt with them is evidence of
engineering judgement and project control. For each significant issue:
what happened, how you diagnosed it, what options you considered, what you
decided and why, and the effect on the product and on the plan. Refer to the
logbook and the updated Gantt chart. Typical length: 300--600 words. With
your supervisor's agreement this can be a section of
\cref{ch:implementation} instead.
\end{guidance}
