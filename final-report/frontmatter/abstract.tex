\addchap{Abstract}

\begin{guidance}[Write this last]
About 250--300 words on a single page. A reader should understand the whole
project from the abstract alone. A common structure is one or two sentences
each on:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the problem and why it matters;
  \item the aim of the project;
  \item what you did (method, design, implementation);
  \item the key results, with numbers where possible;
  \item what the results mean and the main recommendation.
\end{enumerate}
No references, figures or abbreviations that are not explained.
\end{guidance}
