\begin{guidance}[Required evidence (Handbook, Ethics and Health \& Safety)]
The Handbook requires evidence of the following in your Final Report
submission:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the completed DSE self-assessment, using the HSE checklist
    \parencite{hse2013dse};
  \item any additional risk assessments (GRA01) for practical work, approved
    by your supervisor and the technical staff.
\end{itemize}
Including your ethics release from the Solent Ethics App (and the Full
Ethical Review approval, if one was needed) is recommended, and required if
your supervisor asks for it.

Save the PDFs in \texttt{evidence/} with the file names below; they are
included automatically. In final mode, a section whose file is missing is
left out (for example the GRA01 section when there was no practical work),
and a warning in the log tells you so.
\end{guidance}

  {DSE self-assessment (required)}
\evidence{Risk assessment (GRA01)}{evidence/gra01.pdf}
  {GRA01 risk assessment (required if practical work was carried out)}
\evidence{Ethics release}{evidence/ethics-release.pdf}
  {Ethics release from the Solent Ethics App (recommended)}
