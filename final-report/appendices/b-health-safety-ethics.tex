\begin{guidance}[Required evidence (Handbook, Ethics and Health \& Safety)]
Your Final Report submission must include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the completed DSE self-assessment, using the HSE checklist
    \parencite{hse2013dse} -- \textbf{required for everyone};
  \item your ethics release from the Solent Ethics App, and the Full Ethical
    Review approval if one was needed -- \textbf{required for everyone};
  \item any additional risk assessments (GRA01) for practical work, approved
    by your supervisor and the technical staff -- \textbf{required if your
    project included practical work}.
\end{itemize}
Save the PDFs in \texttt{evidence/} with the file names below; they are
included automatically. In final mode, a missing GRA01 is simply left out
(no practical work); a missing DSE assessment or ethics release causes a
clear warning in the log, because the submission is incomplete without it.
\end{guidance}
