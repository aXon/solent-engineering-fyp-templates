\chapter{Project Management Evidence}
\label{app:project-management}

\begin{guidance}
Full-size Gantt chart (original plan and final update), the complete risk
register and, if useful, a summary of supervisor meetings with dates and
agreed actions taken from the logbook. Appendices are not marked as part of
the main body: everything the marker needs to follow the argument must be in
the chapters.

To include a chart exported from other software (Excel, MS Project) as a
landscape page, use for example
\verb|\includepdf[pages=-, landscape]{evidence/gantt.pdf}|.
\end{guidance}
