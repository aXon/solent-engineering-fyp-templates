\section{Review of Phase Two}
\label{sec:review}

\begin{guidance}[What this section does -- about 250 words plus figure and table]
Demonstrates your project monitoring and control so far (Handbook, Phase
Two): the detailed project plan as a Gantt chart with WBS numbers that match
your logbook, a review of progress against the original plan with an
analysis of any deviations, the risk analysis, the resource implications and
the ethical considerations. As a minimum, the Gantt chart shows a
realistic duration (in hours) and phasing of each task, the tasks grouped by
project stage (Proposal, Progress Report, Final Report, Engineering Project
Day), and the actual progress compared with the original plan. A full-size
chart can go in \cref{app:project-plan}.
\end{guidance}

\begin{guidance}
Risk rating = likelihood $\times$ severity, calculated and coloured by
\verb|\riskrating{L}{S}|. Principles: BS ISO 31000 \parencite{bsi2018risk}.
Health and safety hazards of practical work are assessed separately in a
GRA01 form. State the resources you need (equipment, laboratory time,
components, budget) and how they affect the project.
\end{guidance}
