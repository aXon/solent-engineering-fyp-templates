\begin{guidance}
Two or three sentences on the problem and why it matters, then the aim and
the numbered objectives. Write for a visitor who has two minutes.
\end{guidance}

Heating is the largest energy use in UK homes, yet low-cost monitoring of
how a house heats up and cools down is rarely available \parencite{bausch2014}.

\textbf{Aim:} to design and evaluate a low-cost data logger for domestic
heating systems.

\begin{objectives}
  \item Establish requirements from the literature and a market survey.
  \item Generate and select a concept with a weighted decision matrix.
  \item Build and test a prototype against the specification.
\end{objectives}
